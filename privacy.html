Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed); we can never see it.
  • For each child: a first name or nickname, a grade and a level. Please don't enter full names, schools or birthdays.
  • Your province and time zone, so emails arrive after school and skip holidays.
  • A list of the dates we emailed you, so we never send the same day twice.

We don't use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics. The worksheets are made inside your own browser.

Why we use it

Only to run the service: to log you in, show your settings, and send the homework emails you turn on. We never sell or rent your information.

Who helps us

  • Supabase stores accounts and settings.
  • Resend delivers the emails.
  • GitHub hosts the website and runs the daily email job.

These companies may store data outside Canada, including in the United States.

Your choices

  • Turn the daily email off at any time in My account.
  • Delete your account there too. It removes your account, your children and your email history right away.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to correct it, contact us: see the contact address on our home page.

Children

Accounts are for parents and guardians. Children should not create accounts themselves.
