Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The worksheets and the daily email are free while we grow. No credit card, ever.

Do I need an account?

No. Anyone can print worksheets for any grade. An account is only for the daily email and to save each child’s grade and level.

Does it match what my child learns at school?

The questions follow what Canadian children usually learn in each grade: numbers, operations, fractions, measurement, money, reading, grammar and spelling, and the second language.

What about Quebec?

Quebec families get everything in French, from Maternelle to Secondaire 5, with English as the second language. Quebec holidays are skipped for the daily email.

How do I stop the daily email?

Untick it in My account at any time. You can also delete your account, which removes everything.

What do you do with my information?

We only keep your email, your province, and each child’s first name or nickname, grade and level. Nothing is sold or shared. Read the privacy policy.
